These are practical notes for getting to Istanbul From Amsterdam.

I got to the airport 1 hour before the boarding started.
Passing security took 10 minutes (it has never been more than this time).
Passport check took 35 minutes.
15 minutes of walking.
I was pretty on time and when I got there they were boarding.

So getting to Schipol 1 hour before international flights also work. For domestic flights I think 40 minutes before is enough.
Add ten minutes extra in case trains get cancelled or something.

---
For transport form and to the airport I used [Havabus](https://www.havabus.com/yolcuservisi/taksim-sabihagokcenhavalimani.aspx). It costs around 10 euros and if you pay cash it's cheaper than card (4 euro card fee.)
For IST airport havaist works but they accept card only.
It's 1 hour trip to get to city from the airport.

---

I used public transport mostly. Google maps does not know a lot of things. So to avoid wasting your time use the Moovit website.
But there are some things that you can't just check from the phone. For example a bus stop was removed because of construction and there was no signs and the app didn't show the new place. So only talking with (multiple) people can help you.
I also used ferries. It's fast and you can enjoy beautiful views.

---

In this trip I was just walking and talking and seeing the city. So the places I like are for eating:

- Karaköy Güllüoğlu - Nadir Güllü
- Dönerci Hamdi Usta
- 1971Döner

I liked Galataport overall. Somedays there are cruise ships there and you cannot view the sea. but on other days I could sit on the bank and read, and watch birds. I wish they built more places for viewing the sea and less buildings and blockades.

I also went to Büyükada with public ferry, it's around 2 hours. I don't think they are doing a service to this Island. It's all houses that have thick walls and when you look around you don't see that you are on a mountain with a sea view you just see the ally, garbage, and buildings.
But this island has a lot of potential if they can get rid of all the buildings there. There's also a bus there but it just goes around the island and returns. I was looking for something that can take me to the top.
I tried to get to the top on foot but after going for a long time in hot weather I didn't enjoy the view so I returned.
