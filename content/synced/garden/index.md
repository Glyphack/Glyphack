# Unknown Plant

I found this plant on the street along with a lot of other pots.
Most of them was completely dry but this one had small sprouts.
![IMG_4546.jpeg](IMG_4546.jpeg)

I took it and along the way I dropped it. I saw small inspects coming out of the pot.
They had hard shells a body that was narrow at the back and wider near the head with more than 10 legs. Some moving fast and some slow.
![IMG_4551.jpeg](IMG_4551.jpeg)

But anyway I wanted to save this plant so they didn't stop me.
And after I looked it up they were woodlice.
Although its' name is misleading they are not harmful creatures.
They are actually responsible for creating humus in the soil.

I swirled the plant in water before taking it home just to clean all the previous soil.
That soil probably was there for many months.
Now it's in the balcony.
I'm waiting for it to grow to know what it is.
I'm also not sure if this would survive or not given the rough situation it had on the street and other plants were dead.

# Sun Flower

On 2026-06-16 I found this leaf with small spots. Looks like sap sucking pest eggs. It was from my sunflower plant outside.
I cut the leaves that were infected. I sprayed the plant itself. Hoping it would recover.
Sunflowers are more susceptible to pests. Any sunflower that I planted in the last 2 years was infested with pests.
So it requires more care.
