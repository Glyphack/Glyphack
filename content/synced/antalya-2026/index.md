Mostly practical notes for traveling there. The only travel note I have is to definitely visit [Pamukkale](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Pamukkale,+20190+Pamukkale%2FDenizli,+T%C3%BCrkiye/@37.9095018,29.0650301,10478m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m6!3m5!1s0x14c713c95620da2f:0xb1190f5f451efcd6!8m2!3d37.9136957!4d29.1187097!16zL20vMDNuYnNs?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTAxOS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D). It worth the long driving time.

I booked a car and didn't check the location of the rental company.
This was a huge hassle of going to the rental company (30 minute wait time + 20 minute commute) on landing and departure.
It's the stupidity of whoever designed the booking.com car rental page.
It's not enough to just search for "Antalya Airport" location if you want a car. you also have to tick the box that says I want rental office in the terminal.
Otherwise they show you an option for a rental company that is actually somewhere else but is willing to transport you to their office from the airport.
