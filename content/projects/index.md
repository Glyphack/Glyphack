## [Ty](https://github.com/astral-sh/ruff)

I [Contributed ](https://github.com/astral-sh/ruff/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3AGlyphack) to the Ty, a Python type checker by Astral, since November of 2024 during my free time.
In 2025 I [gave a talk](/blog/f-26/) at FOSDEM about the internals of the type checker.

There's also these writings from things I worked on:

- [Running Python tests in Ty](/blog/ty-pytest/)
- [Adding Support for Self to Ty](/blog/ty-self/)
- [Testing in Ty](/blog/ty-test/)

Before that I tried making a [Python type checker](https://github.com/Glyphack/enderpy) from scratch. It helped a lot to gain the required knowledge then [decided](/blog/dv-2/) to put my energy into this project.
My goal was to have a fast python type checker and now it exists!
